    Fan made tool, not affiliated with the game's developers. DPS = base DPS at the chosen upgrade × placed × (1 + 0.8% per level above 1) × (1 + 5% per star) × (Shiny: +10% attack speed) × trait multipliers × self buff uptime (e.g. Frankenstein Apple overcharge) × strongest damage aura × strongest speed aura. Support auras from all four players apply to every player's towers ("With all team buffs"); "With Buff" counts only the buffs inside one loadout. The game data has no support buff numbers, so Support buff values come from the game tables; editable in the AURAS table in this file. Timed buffs (Hot Chili, Frankenstein Apple) show the average of a single tower; staggering several of them raises the real uptime. Average = Buff × (Duration / Cooldown) + 1 × (1 minus Duration / Cooldown). Poison damage assumes one tick per second.
