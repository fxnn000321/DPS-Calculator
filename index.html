    Fan made tool, not affiliated with the game's developers. How DPS is calculated: the base DPS of each upgrade is multiplied by the number of placed towers and by level, star, shiny and trait bonuses. On top of that come the strongest damage and attack speed buffs from support towers. "With all team buffs" counts supports from all four players, "With Buff" only the supports in the same loadout, "Without Buff" none. Timed abilities such as Hot Chili Shake and Frankenstein Apple's Overcharge and range ability are averaged over their cooldown.
